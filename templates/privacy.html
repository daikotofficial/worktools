Privacy

Privacy Policy

Daikot Worktools processes uploaded files only to provide the business workflow requested by the user, including statement analysis, workbook consolidation, template preparation, review summaries, and downloadable outputs.

Files You Upload

Uploaded PDFs and workbooks are used to extract transactions, classify entries, validate totals, prepare templates, or consolidate reports. Generated files are made available for download after processing.

Temporary Processing

Files are stored in the runtime workspace needed to complete analysis and downloads. Hosting platforms may clear temporary storage during restarts, deployments, or maintenance.

Layout Training

When you train an unsupported layout, Daikot Worktools may save reusable layout structure such as detected headers and column roles. This helps similar text-based statements process faster later.

Data Accuracy

The app uses reconciliation checks and review queues to reduce extraction errors. Users remain responsible for reviewing downloaded workbooks before relying on them for financial, tax, or reporting decisions.

Cookies And Analytics

The app may use essential hosting, security, analytics, or advertising technologies if enabled by Daikot. Third-party providers may process limited technical information according to their own policies.

Security

Daikot Worktools is designed to process business documents carefully, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Avoid uploading files you are not authorized to process.
